Questions people ask on the visit.

How much is the deposit, and when do I get it back?

One month's rent for long stays, 15 days for interns. It comes back to your account within 7 days of moving out, minus any damage we've shown you in photos.

Is there a lock-in period?

Three months for long stays, none for intern stays. After that, give us 30 days' notice.

Is the food veg or non-veg?

Both. Every meal has a veg option, and non-veg is served on separate days and counters. Tell us about allergies and the kitchen plans around them.

Can friends or family visit?

Yes, in the lounge between 9 am and 9 pm, signed in at the desk. Parents can stay over in a guest room if one is free.

What do I need to move in?

A government ID, a college or office ID, and a passport photo. You can finish the paperwork online before you arrive.

Can I switch rooms or houses later?

Yes. If your office or college changes, you can move to another Saathi house with the same deposit.
